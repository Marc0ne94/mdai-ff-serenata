# Privacy

Serenata collects no data.

- It makes no network requests and sends nothing anywhere.
- It reads no files except the song bundled with it, `audio/canzone.m4a`.
- It stores one setting, whether the band above the prompt is on, in Claude
  Code's own plugin storage on your computer.

To remove that setting, uninstall the plugin (see the README).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Marc0ne94/mdai-serenata/issues
