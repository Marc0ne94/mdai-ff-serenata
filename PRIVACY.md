# Privacy

Serenata collects no data.

- It makes no network requests and sends nothing anywhere.
- It reads no files except the songs bundled with it, in its `audio` folder.
- It stores a few settings in Claude Code's own plugin storage on your
  computer: whether the band above the prompt is on, which song is picked, the
  song picked last in each collection, the sound and voice picked for each
  song, and whether the settings are shown.

To remove those settings, uninstall the plugin (see the README).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Marc0ne94/mdai-ff-serenata/issues
